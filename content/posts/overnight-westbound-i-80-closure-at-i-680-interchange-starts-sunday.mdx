Westbound Interstate 80 will fully close overnight at the Interstate 680 interchange in Omaha for four nights starting Sunday, the Nebraska Department of Transportation said.

The closure runs from 9 p.m. to 6 a.m. each night from Sunday, Nov. 15, through Wednesday, Nov. 18, weather permitting, covering reference posts 447 to 445. Westbound drivers will be diverted to the I-L-Q Street ramp at [Exit 445](/cities/omaha), according to NDOT's Nov. 13 release.

## What we know

- **Location:** Westbound I-80 at the I-680 interchange ([Exit 446](/cities/omaha), mile 446.0), between reference posts 447 and 445 in Omaha. The signed detour uses the I-L-Q Street ramp (Exit 445).
- **Time:** 9 p.m. to 6 a.m. nightly, Sunday Nov. 15 through Wednesday Nov. 18, weather permitting. NDOT announced the schedule on Nov. 13.
- **Status:** Upcoming planned closure. Nebraska 511 event CARSx-3661 (retrieved Nov. 14) lists a matching overnight closure between Exit 448 (84th Street) and Exit 445 (L Street) for the same pavement work, starting the night of Nov. 15.
- **Agency:** Nebraska Department of Transportation, District 2. Contractor is Iowa Civil Contracting of Victor, Iowa. Contact listed: Barb Gerbino-Bevins, 402-595-2534.

The work includes pavement repairs, diamond grinding, and penetrating sealants, NDOT said. Diamond grinding smooths the concrete surface and sealants protect it — standard preservation work that requires dry conditions, which is why the schedule is weather permitting.

## Why it matters

This is a full westbound closure, not a lane narrowing. During work hours, through drivers cannot stay on westbound I-80 past the I-680 interchange and must take the signed detour via the I-L-Q ramp. Overnight truck traffic through Omaha is heavy, and a Sunday-to-Wednesday window means four consecutive weeknights of disruption.

The location compounds the impact. Exits 445, 446, and 448 sit close together in west Omaha, where I-80, I-680, US-275, and the 84th Street interchange feed into each other. Drivers headed to Elkhorn, Gretna, or west toward Lincoln should expect slower speeds and merging traffic around the detour. See the [Omaha exit guide](/news/i-80-omaha-exits-river-crossing) for how exits 445–448 connect.

This closure is separate from the [nighttime lane closures at mile 427](/news/nighttime-lane-closures-start-sunday-on-i-80-at-mile-427) between Lincoln and Omaha, which involve alternating lane closures for bridge joint replacement. The I-680 work is a full directional closure with a detour.

## What happens next

NDOT said the work runs nightly through Wednesday, Nov. 18 — meaning the final closure ends the morning of Thursday, Nov. 19 — if weather holds. Rain or cold could shift nights. Before driving, check [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for the current closure status and the posted detour map, and read our guide on [how to read Nebraska 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511) for overnight work zones.
