Five southeast Nebraska law enforcement agencies are teaming up for joint roadway enforcement around York and Saline counties, including the I-80 corridor at York, the Nebraska State Patrol announced Thursday.

## What we know

- **What:** The RIDER (Rural Impaired Driving Enforcement and Reduction) Task Force was announced Nov. 19 at the Holthus Convention Center in York, according to the [Nebraska State Patrol](https://statepatrol.nebraska.gov/nsp-local-agencies-launch-rider-task-force).
- **Who:** The York and Saline County Sheriff's Offices, the York and Crete police departments and the Nebraska State Patrol.
- **How:** RIDER officers will be cross-deputized between communities so deputies and officers can patrol in both York and Saline counties, alongside troopers' statewide jurisdiction, with joint enforcement operations in each county, NSP said.
- **Why:** In the past five years, troopers, deputies and officers responded to more than 2,400 crashes in the two counties, with 20 deaths and over 100 serious injuries, according to NSP. [KLKN reports](https://www.klkntv.com/new-rider-task-force-targets-impaired-driving/) the agencies made a combined 197 DUI arrests in 2025 alone.
- **Funding:** The task force is supported by grant funding from the Nebraska Department of Transportation Highway Safety Office.

## Why it matters

York sits at mile 353 on I-80 at the US-81 interchange, and Saline County straddles the Lincoln–York stretch — both squarely in the Thanksgiving travel path. "By combining personnel, equipment, experience, and expertise, we can increase our presence on the roadway," York County Sheriff's Sgt. Taylor Samek said in the NSP release.

The emphasis is impaired driving, but the mission covers speeding, aggressive and distracted driving and seat-belt use, according to KLKN's report from the announcement. With Thanksgiving a week out, MADD Nebraska is also reminding drivers that an impaired-driving decision "will turn celebration into heartbreak," program director Ashley Rowe told KLKN.

## What happens next

Expect coordinated patrols and targeted traffic-safety operations in both counties through the holiday season. Officials say the goal is prevention — fewer crashes, injuries and deaths — not simply more arrests or citations. Drivers on I-80 between Lincoln and York should plan for visible enforcement and, as always, a sober ride.
