Kearney is a natural midpoint stop. It has the last full-service hospital before North Platte, and it is the point where a drive across Nebraska stops feeling like a suburb and starts feeling like the plains.

For travelers, Kearney is a decision point: stop, fuel up and check the weather, or push west into the 95-mile gap to North Platte.

## The exits

**Mile 272 — Kearney's south side.** The main approach from the interstate into the city, with fuel, food and hotel clusters nearby. This is the exit most travelers use.

**Mile 275 — central Kearney.** Another local access point, convenient for the hospital and the university area.

**Mile 279 — east Kearney.** The eastern edge of the city, serving the industrial and retail development along the interstate.

## Why Kearney matters on the corridor

- **Health care.** CHI Health Good Samaritan is a regional referral hospital. If you are injured west of Kearney, this is often where you are taken.
- **Education.** The University of Nebraska at Kearney brings events, students and traffic to the city.
- **Services.** Fuel, food, hotels, repairs and retail are clustered here in a way they are not again until North Platte.
- **The Archway.** The Great Platte River Road Archway, just east of town, is the corridor's best-known roadside stop.

## The weather split

Kearney sits in the transition zone between eastern Nebraska's moisture and the drier, windier west. That means it can get the eastern corridor's ice and the western corridor's ground blizzards in the same week.

The National Weather Service office in [Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) covers the Kearney area. When a winter storm is forecast, the timing for Kearney is often the difference between a manageable drive and a closed interstate at Lexington or North Platte.

## What to check before heading west

1. [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for the segment from Kearney to North Platte, including Lexington and Gothenburg.
2. The [NWS Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) forecast and any winter storm warning.
3. Fuel. Fill up in Kearney or Lexington; do not assume the smaller exits are open.
4. The [State Patrol newsroom](https://statepatrol.nebraska.gov/divisions/office-superintendent/news) for any confirmed incident.

Kearney is also a good place to stop for the night if the western forecast is deteriorating. The next full-service city is 95 miles away.
