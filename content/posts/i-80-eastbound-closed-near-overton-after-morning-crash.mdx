Interstate 80 is closed eastbound near Overton on Wednesday morning after a crash, with all eastbound lanes shut between mile marker 247 and Exit 248, about 10 miles east of Lexington, according to the Nebraska Department of Transportation.

A second eastbound crash roughly six miles farther east, between mile markers 253 and 256 near the Elm Creek exit, is blocking the right lane, Nebraska 511 reports. Both listings are from Wednesday morning; the closure was updated at 5:37 a.m. CST and the lane blockage at 5:23 a.m. CST.

## What we know

- **Where:** The full closure runs from mile marker 247 to [Exit 248 (NE Link 24B) at Overton](/cities/lexington), in the eastbound direction. The lane-blockage crash is between mile markers 253 and 256, just west of Exit 257 (U.S. 183) at Elm Creek. Both are on the [Lexington-to-Kearney stretch](/news/lexington-at-mile-237-head-to-head-traffic-in-farm-country) in Dawson and Buffalo counties.
- **When:** Wednesday morning, Nov. 18. The lane blockage was first posted at about 4:22 a.m. CST; both listings were updated between 5:23 and 5:37 a.m. CST.
- **What:** Nebraska DOT lists the Overton event as a road closure caused by a crash, with all eastbound lanes closed, and the mile 253–256 event as a crash with the right lane blocked. Nebraska 511 does not report a cause, and no injury information has been released.
- **Status:** As of 5:37 a.m. CST, eastbound I-80 was fully closed at Overton with the right lane blocked near Elm Creek. Closures on this corridor often clear within hours, so recheck [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) before leaving — 511 notes detour information on its map for the closure.
- **Who confirmed it:** The [Nebraska Department of Transportation](https://511.nebraska.gov/), via Nebraska 511. The State Patrol newsroom shows no release on either crash as of Wednesday morning.

## Why it matters

This is the morning commute and freight window on one of the busiest rural segments of I-80 in Nebraska, the direct route between [Lexington](/cities/lexington) and Kearney. A full eastbound closure here backs traffic up fast, and the only nearby eastbound exits to absorb diverted drivers are Lexington (Exit 237, U.S. 283) and Overton (Exit 248) — check the 511 map rather than guessing at a county-road detour.

Drivers should also expect a gusty afternoon. The [National Weather Service in Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) has a Red Flag Warning in effect from noon to 6 p.m. CST Wednesday for Dawson, Buffalo and surrounding counties, with west winds around 20 mph gusting to 25–30 mph and humidity as low as 20 percent. That warning is about fire weather, not road conditions, but crosswinds of that strength are felt most by high-profile vehicles, campers and empty trailers — exactly the traffic that fills this Dawson County freight corridor. No official has linked the wind to either morning crash; the cause of both crashes is unknown.

## What happens next

Watch Nebraska 511 for the reopening: when the Overton closure lifts, NDOT will remove or update the listing, and any injury or cause details would typically come later from the State Patrol. If you are heading east from Lexington or North Platte this morning, check conditions first with our [guide to reading 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511) and allow extra time — and if you are hauling through Dawson County, note the [planned I-80 truck parking expansion](/news/usdot-backs-120-new-i-80-truck-parking-stalls-in-dawson-county) that underscores how heavy truck traffic is on exactly this stretch.
