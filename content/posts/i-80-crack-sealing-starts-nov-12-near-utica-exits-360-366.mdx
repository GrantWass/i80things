Drivers between York and Seward should expect intermittent lane closures on I-80 starting Thursday: crack sealing from reference post 359.72 to 365.14 begins Nov. 12, weather permitting, covering the Waco overhead bridge and the Utica interchange in both directions.

According to the [Nebraska Department of Transportation's District 4 release](https://dot.nebraska.gov/media/4yvf0fu1/20261102-d4-42949.pdf), issued Nov. 2 from Grand Island, the contractor is Northwest Asphalt & Maintenance of Thief River Falls, Minnesota. [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/event/CARSx-4486) describes the zone as mile marker 358 to Exit 366 at Utica, with intermittent lane closures starting at 7 a.m. CST on Nov. 12 and a 14-foot width restriction in effect.

## What we know

- **Where:** I-80 in both directions from reference post 359.72 to 365.14 — the Waco overhead bridge at [Exit 360](/news/york-at-mile-353-the-balloon-water-tower-at-the-i-80-us-81-crossroads) through the Utica interchange at Exit 366, in Seward County between York and Seward.
- **When:** Starting Thursday, Nov. 12, at 7 a.m. CST, weather permitting. Anticipated completion is March 2027.
- **Status:** Scheduled and confirmed by NDOT District 4 on Nov. 2. Traffic will be maintained with lane closures; no detour is posted.
- **Agency:** Nebraska Department of Transportation, District 4 (contact Jerrid Fuehrer, 402-710-6500).

## Why it matters

This is a short but busy farm-belt stretch. The five-mile zone sits between the US-81 junction at York, which funnels north-south ag and freight traffic onto the interstate, and the Seward County exits that serve Beaver Crossing, Goehner and the county seat. Grain trucks, wide farm equipment and regular commuter traffic between Lincoln and Grand Island all share these lanes, so even an intermittent single-lane squeeze can stack up traffic at harvest-season pace.

The 14-foot width limit deserves attention from anyone hauling wide loads: standard interstate lanes are 12 feet, and a 14-foot restriction leaves little margin for oversize equipment. If your load is close to that width, check with NDOT before rolling through.

Crack sealing itself is preventive maintenance — sealing joints and cracks in the pavement surface before water gets in, freezes and breaks the road apart over winter. It is the kind of work NDOT schedules in the shoulder season precisely because freeze-thaw is coming. Expect cones, arrow boards and pilot pacing rather than a full closure, and expect the crew to move along the zone rather than sit in one spot.

## What happens next

With completion set for March 2027, the work will span the winter months, when cold, snow and wind routinely pause this kind of paving-adjacent work. Treat the end date as approximate and watch for the zone to go quiet during bad weather, then reappear.

Before driving it, check [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for the current lane status; our [guide to reading 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511) explains the map symbols. For the bigger construction picture, see [where NDOT posts I-80 construction](/news/ndot-i-80-construction-nebraska) and the overnight work also underway at [mile 427 between Lincoln and Omaha](/news/nighttime-lane-closures-start-sunday-on-i-80-at-mile-427). NDOT's standing advice applies in every work zone: slow down, buckle up and put the phone down.
