Grand Island is the biggest city on I-80 between Lincoln and Kearney, and it drives like one: four interchanges, rail and manufacturing truck traffic, hospital traffic and, in late summer, State Fair traffic. Check Nebraska 511 for the current work pattern before you cross town; construction moves faster than a guide can.

For travelers, Grand Island is the corridor's midpoint metro — the place to stop for services, and the junction for Hastings to the south.

## The exits

**Mile 305 — Alda and west Grand Island.** The western approach, using NE Link 40C to Alda and the industrial and retail strip on the city's west edge.

**Mile 312 — Grand Island, Hastings and US-281.** The key interchange. US-281 runs south from here to Hastings, about 16 miles by road — a real detour, not a quick off-ramp, so budget the extra time.

**Mile 314 — Locust Street.** The central/east access to the city's core, hospital district and local streets.

**Mile 318 — Nebraska 2, Phillips and east Grand Island.** The eastern edge and the fast way back onto open highway toward Lincoln.

## Work zones

NDOT and [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) are the source for current lane closures and maintenance around Grand Island. Work can shift between exits 305, 312, 314 and 318, so check the map before crossing town, especially at night or in fog. Merge early, hold the posted speed and give maintenance crews room.

## Why Grand Island matters on the corridor

- **The State Fair.** The [Nebraska State Fair](https://www.statefair.org/) runs at Fonner Park on the city's north side. The fair says more than 300,000 guests attended over 11 days in 2026, with the next fair set for August 27–September 6, 2027. Event days change the traffic pattern at every Grand Island exit.
- **The Hastings spur.** Hastings — the Adams County seat, a regional hospital town and the birthplace of Kool-Aid — sits south via US-281 from [Exit 312](/exits/312), about 16 miles by road. If your business is in Hastings, this is your exit.
- **Freight town.** Hall County's rail and manufacturing base puts work trucks and grain haulers on the local roads, especially during planting and harvest. Expect them on the US-281 approaches.
- **Weather transition.** The [NWS Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) office covers this stretch. Early November brings valley fog and the first ice risk of the season; check the forecast before you drive, because visibility can drop quickly between Grand Island and Kearney.

## What to check before driving

1. [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for current work around exits 305–318.
2. The [NWS Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) forecast and any fog or winter advisory — valley fog can drop visibility to near zero between Grand Island and Kearney.
3. Fuel and food are easy here; this is one of the corridor's densest service clusters. If Hastings is your destination, turn south at Exit 312 with a full tank.
4. The [State Fair schedule](https://www.statefair.org/) in late August — fair week means heavier traffic at all four exits.
