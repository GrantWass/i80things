Grand Island is the biggest city on I-80 between Lincoln and Kearney, and it drives like one: four interchanges, rail and manufacturing truck traffic, hospital traffic and, in late summer, State Fair traffic. Right now there is also bridge work on the eastbound side near mile 310.

For travelers, Grand Island is the corridor's midpoint metro — the place to stop for services, and the junction for Hastings to the south.

## The exits

**Mile 306 — west Grand Island.** The western approach, serving the industrial and retail strip on the city's west edge.

**Mile 309 — central access.** Local access toward the city's core and the hospital district.

**Mile 312 — Grand Island, Hastings and US-281.** The key interchange. US-281 runs south from here to Hastings, about 25 miles off the interstate — a real detour, not a quick off-ramp, so budget the extra time.

**Mile 314 — east Grand Island.** The eastern edge and the fast way back onto open highway toward Lincoln.

## The work near mile 310

According to Nebraska 511, two active items sit on top of Grand Island's exits:

- **Eastbound bridge work** between mile 310 and Exit 312, with the right lane closed in the eastbound direction.
- **Road maintenance** between mile 310 and the Highway 2 junction east of town, with a slow-moving maintenance vehicle in the work area.

Neither is a closure, but both reward attention: merge early eastbound approaching Exit 312, and give the maintenance crew room. Check the [current 511 map](https://511.nebraska.gov/) before crossing town, especially at night or in fog.

## Why Grand Island matters on the corridor

- **The State Fair.** The [Nebraska State Fair](https://www.statefair.org/) runs at Fonner Park on the city's north side. The fair says more than 300,000 guests attended over 11 days in 2026, with the next fair set for August 27–September 6, 2027. Event days change the traffic pattern at every Grand Island exit.
- **The Hastings spur.** Hastings — the Adams County seat, a regional hospital town and the birthplace of Kool-Aid — sits south via US-281 from Exit 312. If your business is in Hastings, this is your exit.
- **Freight town.** Hall County's rail and manufacturing base puts work trucks and grain haulers on the local roads, especially during planting and harvest. Expect them on the US-281 approaches.
- **Weather transition.** The [NWS Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) office covers this stretch. Early November brings valley fog and the first ice risk of the season; check the forecast before you drive, because visibility can drop quickly between Grand Island and Kearney.

## What to check before driving

1. [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for the mile 310–312 bridge work and the maintenance zone east of town.
2. The [NWS Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/) forecast and any fog or winter advisory — valley fog can drop visibility to near zero between Grand Island and Kearney.
3. Fuel and food are easy here; this is one of the corridor's densest service clusters. If Hastings is your destination, turn south at Exit 312 with a full tank.
4. The [State Fair schedule](https://www.statefair.org/) in late August — fair week means heavier traffic at all four exits.
