A Wind Advisory covers the eastern third of I-80 in Nebraska through 4 p.m. CST Friday, with south winds strong enough to push around high-profile vehicles, according to the National Weather Service in Omaha/Valley.

## What we know

- **Where:** The advisory includes Douglas, Sarpy, Saunders, Seward, Lancaster and Cass counties — the eastern I-80 counties from the Seward area east to Omaha, in both directions.
- **When:** In effect from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. CST Friday, Nov. 20. The alert was issued at 9:55 p.m. CST Thursday, Nov. 19, by [NWS Omaha/Valley](https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active?point=41.2565,-95.9345).
- **What:** South winds of 22 to 32 mph with peak gusts up to 49 mph, according to the alert. NWS warns gusty winds will blow around unsecured objects and could bring down small tree limbs.
- **Road status:** [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) showed no full I-80 closures in eastern Nebraska on Friday morning. A [westbound guardrail repair near Grand Island (mile 310)](https://511.nebraska.gov/event/CARSx-4800) has the left lane closed, and long-term work zones remain active near [Milford–Lincoln](https://511.nebraska.gov/event/CARSx-210) and at the [I-80/I-180 interchange (exit 401)](https://511.nebraska.gov/event/CARSx-2231).

## Why it matters

Crosswinds in the 40 to 50 mph range are a handling problem on open interstate, especially for box trucks, RVs, livestock trailers and empty semis. The exposed stretches west of Omaha and across Saunders and Seward counties offer little windbreak, and gusts can arrive in bursts rather than as a steady push.

Drivers should slow down, keep both hands on the wheel, allow extra following distance and secure any loose cargo. NWS specifically advises extra caution for high-profile vehicles.

## What happens next

The advisory expires at 4 p.m. Friday. [KMTV 3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/weather/windy-warm-friday-then-a-cold-front-brings-rain-and-colder-air-saturday) reports Friday will be warm — near 70 degrees in Omaha — before a cold front brings rain Saturday and colder air for Thanksgiving week. [KLKN](https://www.klkntv.com/warm-and-windy-friday-rain-and-cooler-saturday/) and [WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2026/11/20/rustys-first-alert-6-forecast-gusty-wind-incredible-warmth/) carry similar forecasts for the Lincoln–Omaha corridor.

Check current conditions before leaving: [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for closures and cameras, and the [NWS Omaha/Valley](https://www.weather.gov/oax/) page for updates. The desk will update this story if the advisory is extended or a closure is posted.
