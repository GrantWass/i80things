Hastings is the biggest city on the corridor that is not actually on the interstate. It sits south of Grand Island, reached by US-281 from I-80 [Exit 312](/exits/312), and the drive is long enough that you should treat it as a destination rather than a quick off-ramp.

## The spur

**Exit 312 — Grand Island, Hastings and US-281.** From I-80, take US-281 south. The highway runs through farmland and the Platte valley before reaching Hastings, roughly 16 miles by road; Hastings College's own directions use that figure. The trip is straightforward, but it is not a five-minute detour; budget the time and check the weather before you commit.

The other Grand Island exits serve Grand Island itself. If Hastings is your destination, Exit 312 is the one that matters.

## The city

Hastings is the Adams County seat and the principal city of the Hastings micropolitan area. The 2020 census counted 25,152 people. It is a regional center for health care, manufacturing, retail and education, not a bedroom community.

Downtown Hastings runs along Burlington Avenue and the surrounding blocks, with the older commercial buildings, the courthouse and the city's civic core. The city announced a Nov. 2, 2026 ribbon cutting for its renovated City Hall at 615 W. 3rd St. The project moved city offices back into the building after the renovation.

Hastings College is in the city. Central Community College also has a Hastings campus, housed partly in buildings from the former Naval Ammunition Depot. The depot was the largest of four inland U.S. Navy ammunition depots during World War II, and Hastings was designated an American World War II Heritage City in 2023.

## Kool-Aid and the museum

Edwin Perkins invented Kool-Aid in Hastings in 1927, turning a liquid concentrate called Fruit Smack into a powder that was cheaper to ship. The city celebrates that history with Kool-Aid Days each August; the 2026 festival was scheduled for Aug. 14–16.

The Hastings Museum of Natural and Cultural History is the other reason to build in time. The building, completed in 1939, houses the McDonald Planetarium, a large diorama of sandhill and whooping cranes, and exhibits on the region's natural and cultural history. It is a genuine museum, not a roadside curiosity.

## Why Hastings matters on the corridor

- **The US-281 connection.** Hastings is the main city south of the Grand Island interchange. For travelers heading to Adams County, the museum, the college or the hospital, Exit 312 is the gateway.
- **A regional center.** Hastings has services that the smaller interstate towns do not: a hospital, a college and a downtown with actual blocks.
- **The Kool-Aid connection.** The invention story is specific, local and verifiable. It is also a useful reminder that the corridor's identity is not only crashes and closures.
- **Weather.** The NWS Hastings office is in Hastings, and it covers the city and the surrounding region. In winter, the drive back north to I-80 can be the part that changes fastest.
