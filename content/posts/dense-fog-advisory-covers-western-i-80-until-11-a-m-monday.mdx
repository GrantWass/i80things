A dense fog advisory is in effect for the western Nebraska stretch of Interstate 80 on Monday morning, with visibility possibly dropping to a quarter-mile or less around Chappell and Ogallala, according to the National Weather Service in North Platte.

The advisory runs from 1 a.m. to 11 a.m. CST Monday, Nov. 16 (midnight to 10 a.m. MST), and covers Deuel, Garden, Chase, Keith, Perkins, Arthur, Grant, Hooker and McPherson counties.

## What we know

- **Where:** On [I-80 near Ogallala](/cities/ogallala), the advisory covers Deuel and Keith counties — roughly from Exit 85 (Chappell) east through Exit 107 (Big Springs), Exit 126 (Ogallala) and Exit 133 (Roscoe). The other listed counties sit off the interstate to the north and south.
- **When:** In effect 1 a.m. to 11 a.m. CST Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. The advisory was issued at 7:39 p.m. CST Sunday, Nov. 15.
- **What:** Visibility of one-quarter mile or less in dense fog, the weather service said.
- **Status:** Nebraska 511 showed no full I-80 closures in the advisory area as of early Monday morning — only previously scheduled construction, including the long-term work zone near Ogallala. Conditions can change quickly in fog, so recheck before leaving.
- **Who confirmed it:** The [NWS North Platte office](https://www.weather.gov/lbf/), via its active-alerts feed.

## Why it matters

This is the Monday-morning commute window for truckers, school buses and travelers heading through the Panhandle gateway. The fog zone overlaps the long-term [Ogallala work zone](/news/ogallala-at-mile-126-lake-gateway-in-an-i-80-work-zone) (mile markers 124–133), where the eastbound speed limit is 65 mph with an 11-foot width restriction in effect, according to Nebraska 511 — a combination that leaves little margin when visibility collapses. Fog is also hardest to see coming: a clear on-ramp can turn into a white wall within a mile.

The weather service's driving guidance is simple: slow down, use headlights (not high beams, which reflect back in fog), and leave plenty of distance ahead. See our [winter driving guide](/news/winter-driving-i-80-nebraska) for stopping-distance and whiteout basics that apply to fog as well.

## What happens next

The advisory expires at 11 a.m. CST Monday. If fog lingers or crashes close lanes, the weather service can extend it and NDOT will post closures to [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) — check conditions there, and our [guide to reading 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511), before driving west of North Platte on Monday morning.
