Driving west on I-80 through Omaha, the exit signs go 445, 446, 445.

It is not a misprint. Exit 445 is assigned to two different interchanges more than two miles apart, and [exit 446](/exits/446) sits between them.

## What the numbers actually point to

The Nebraska reference log lists three separate locations under those two numbers:

- **Exit 445 — Q Street**, at mile 444.6. Westbound exit only, reached from the collector-distributor lanes.
- **Exit 446 — I-680**, at mile 445.97.
- **Exit 445 — West Center Road**, at mile 446.6. No eastbound exit; this is also I-680 exit 1.

Between them, the US-275/NE-92 (L Street) ramps at mile 445.05 and the I Street ramps at 445.34 are part of the same lane complex. They carry no separate exit number.

So the number 445 brackets the I-680 interchange on both sides, with 446 in the middle.

The sequence is directional. Westbound, all three appear: West Center Road, then I-680, then Q Street. Eastbound, neither 445 ramp is available — Q Street is westbound-only and West Center Road has no eastbound exit — so a driver heading toward Iowa sees only 446 in this stretch, with the C/D lanes serving entrances at L and I Streets.

## Why the duplicate exists

This is the collector-distributor — "C/D" — section of I-80. Instead of every local street getting its own interchange on the mainline, I-80 splits into a main roadway and a pair of parallel lanes that serve several streets in sequence. The parallel lanes let local traffic leave and enter without weaving across three lanes of through traffic.

Exit numbers track the mile where a ramp leaves the mainline. In this stretch, the local ramps at both ends of the C/D complex kept the 445 number, while I-680 — the big interchange in the middle — is 446. The reference log notes the C/D lanes provide access to the I, L and Q Street exits, and that the Q Street exit is reached from the lanes that begin at West Center Road.

## Why it matters

Most days, none of this matters. It matters when someone gives you directions, or when you are reading an incident report:

- "Take exit 445" is ambiguous. Q Street and West Center Road are 2.1 miles apart, on opposite sides of I-680.
- A 511 entry that says "exit 445" may mean either one. The street name is the only reliable detail.
- If you take the first 445 you see and you wanted the other one, you are already past I-680.

## The rest of the corridor

Everywhere else in Nebraska, the exit number tracks the mile marker. Exit 439 is at mile 439.2, [exit 449](/exits/449) at 449.3, [exit 454](/exits/454) at 454.1. The mile markers run from 0 at the Wyoming line to 455.3 at the Missouri River bridge.

The Omaha C/D section is the exception, and it is a design choice rather than a mistake: one number for a pair of local ramps, one number for the freeway-to-freeway interchange between them.

## What to say instead

Use the street name: "Q Street" or "West Center Road," not "exit 445." For I-680, the number is unambiguous — 446.

If you are reading this because a navigation app told you to take exit 445 and you are not sure which one, the street name on the sign is the tiebreaker. The number alone is not.
