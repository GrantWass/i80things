Both on-ramps from Pacific Street to Interstate 680 in west Omaha are open again, the Nebraska Department of Transportation said Nov. 24, restoring a west-metro connection just as Thanksgiving travelers head home.

## What we know

- **Location:** The northbound and southbound on-ramps from Pacific Street to I-680 in west Omaha. I-680 meets I-80 at Exit 446 (mile 445.97), linking the Pacific Street interchange back into the Omaha interstate system.
- **Time:** NDOT announced the reopening Nov. 24. Lane restoration on I-680 continues over the next three weeks, weather permitting.
- **Status:** Ramps open. Remaining lane work happens overnight with traffic control devices, according to NDOT District 2.
- **Agency:** Nebraska Department of Transportation, District 2 ([release 20261124-D2-22852](https://dot.nebraska.gov/media/oigehwbb/20261124-d2-22852.pdf)). Contractor is Hawkins Construction of Omaha.

Remaining work includes installing new cameras, seeding, roadside markers and signs, plus permanent durable striping. NDOT said the final striping will wait for spring 2027, when overnight temperatures are warm enough for the material to cure. Some of that close-out work happens off the roadway; the rest runs overnight.

## Why it matters

Pacific Street is one of the main east-west arterials feeding I-680 on Omaha's west side, and I-680 is the western bypass that rejoins I-80 at Exit 446. With the ramps closed, drivers had to divert to nearby I-680 entries during construction. The reopening lands ahead of the Thanksgiving weekend return, when west Omaha traffic funnels toward I-80 eastbound and the I-680 interchange.

This is distinct from the overnight westbound I-80 work at the I-680 interchange earlier in November — see our [earlier Omaha overnight closure story](/news/overnight-westbound-i-80-closure-at-i-680-interchange-starts-sunday). That project closed through lanes; this one restores access ramps. Drivers crossing Omaha should still expect overnight lane shifts on I-680 for up to three weeks while crews restore the original lane configuration and open the new lanes.

For the broader metro picture, our [guide to Omaha's I-80 exits](/news/i-80-omaha-exits-river-crossing) maps how Exits 452, 446 and 445 fit together, and our [guide to Nebraska 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511) walks through closure and camera listings.

## What happens next

Watch for overnight lane changes on I-680 near Pacific Street over the next three weeks. NDOT said the schedule is weather permitting, so rain or freezing temperatures could shift nights. Final durable striping is scheduled for spring 2027.

Before driving, check the [Nebraska 511 map](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for any active I-680 or I-80 listings in the Omaha metro. More Omaha corridor news lives on the [Omaha city hub](/cities/omaha).
