The ramp from northbound U.S. Highway 75 to westbound Interstate 80 in Omaha closes overnight Thursday, the Nebraska Department of Transportation said, as state crews repair the ramp surface.

## What we know

- **Location:** The full length of the northbound US-75 ramp to westbound I-80, at the US-75 interchange with I-80 in Omaha (Exit 452, mile ~452.8).
- **Time:** 10 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 19, to 5 a.m. Friday, Nov. 20, Central Time, weather permitting.
- **Status:** A full ramp closure. State maintenance forces will perform surface road repairs on the interchange, with traffic maintained by traffic control devices, according to NDOT's District 2 release.
- **Agency:** Nebraska Department of Transportation, District 2 ([release 20261118-D2](https://dot.nebraska.gov/media/tnbbustv/20261118-d2.pdf)).

NDOT's Nov. 18 correction release schedules the work for "Thursday, Nov. 19 from 10 p.m. to 5 a.m.," correcting the original release's Wednesday date. If you saw the first version, go with Thursday night.

## Why it matters

This is the ramp that carries Kennedy Freeway traffic onto westbound I-80 toward 72nd Street, the I-680 interchange, Elkhorn and on out of the metro. An overnight window keeps daytime traffic clear, but anyone driving late Thursday — including freight running the overnight hours — will need an alternate way onto westbound I-80. NDOT did not publish a signed detour for this work, describing it as maintenance with traffic control in place, so expect cones, narrowed approaches and possible short delays near the interchange.

The timing overlaps with a separate run of overnight westbound I-80 closures at the I-680 interchange (Exit 446) that NDOT scheduled Nov. 15–18 — see our [earlier Omaha overnight closure story](/news/overnight-westbound-i-80-closure-at-i-680-interchange-starts-sunday). Drivers crossing Omaha late Thursday should check conditions on both ends of the westbound lanes. Our [guide to Omaha's I-80 exits](/news/i-80-omaha-exits-river-crossing) maps how Exits 452, 446 and 445 fit together.

## What happens next

The work is scheduled for a single night and should wrap by 5 a.m. Friday, weather permitting. If rain or cold moves in, maintenance work like this typically slides to the next available night — NDOT has not announced a backup date.

Before leaving, check the [Nebraska 511 map](https://511.nebraska.gov/) for the closure listing and any detour posted overnight.
