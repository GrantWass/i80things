Thanksgiving return traffic on Interstate 80 in Nebraska gets wet before it gets cold: National Weather Service forecasts checked Friday morning, Nov. 27, show rain reaching all 432 Nebraska miles of I-80 — Omaha (mile 454) to Kimball (mile 22) — during Saturday, Nov. 28, with a rain-and-snow mix possible at the Wyoming end and a hard freeze behind it that could leave slick spots for early Sunday drivers.

Friday itself is the dry travel day. NWS gridpoint forecasts show highs near 50 around Omaha, in the mid-50s from Kearney through North Platte, and near 56 at the Wyoming end — but Cheyenne warns of northwest gusts up to 45 mph on Friday near Kimball, worth noting for high-profile vehicles. The rain arrives Saturday from west to east through the afternoon and evening, then exits, leaving Sunday colder, blustery and dry.

## What we know

- **Timing and coverage:** Per NWS point forecasts pulled Friday morning, rain is likely after noon Saturday near Omaha (70% chance), rain is expected Saturday near Hastings and Kearney (80%), rain showers are likely Saturday near North Platte (high near 43), and rain and snow showers are likely Saturday near Kimball (high near 44). Amounts run generally a tenth to a half inch — enough to wet the interstate end to end, not enough to flood it.
- **Snow potential in the west:** The [North Platte office's zone](https://www.weather.gov/lbf/) shows a chance of snow showers mixing in Saturday morning before rain takes over, and the [Cheyenne office's zone](https://www.weather.gov/cys/) shows rain and snow showers likely through the day Saturday near the state line, with rain showers likely Friday night first. Minor accumulations under an inch are possible on grass; snow on wet pavement will be slow to stick, according to the 10/11 KOLN forecast, which matches the NWS grids.
- **The Sunday freeze:** Saturday-night lows fall to about 35 near Omaha, 29 in south-central Nebraska, 24 around North Platte and 19 near Kimball, with clearing skies and northwest winds gusting around 17 mph near Omaha and up
to 40 mph farther west. As [10/11's Bill Rentschler notes](https://www.1011now.com/2026/11/26/milder-temperatures-return-friday-before-saturday-rain-chances/), wet roads could freeze where temperatures drop and moisture lingers — plan for slick bridges and overpasses if you roll early Sunday. [KMTV Omaha](https://www.3newsnow.com/weather/cloudy-and-cold-friday-tracking-widespread-rain-on-saturday) gives the same caution for the metro.
- **No alerts, no closures — yet:** No NWS watches, warnings or advisories were active at the Omaha, Kearney, North Platte or Kimball corridor points as of Friday morning, and [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/) showed no I-80 crashes or closures — only the standing long-term construction zones, including Cozad to Darr (mile markers 219-231), Ogallala (mile markers 124-133), the Lincoln widening (mile markers 382-395) and the I-180 interchange (mile markers 400-401).
- **Who confirmed it:** The four [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/oax/) forecast offices serving the corridor, via their public gridpoint forecasts checked Friday morning, Nov. 27, consistent with the Thanksgiving-weekend outlooks from [10/11 KOLN](https://www.1011now.com/2026/11/26/milder-temperatures-return-friday-before-saturday-rain-chances/) and [KMTV 3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/weather/cloudy-and-cold-friday-tracking-widespread-rain-on-saturday). Road status is from [Nebraska 511](https://511.nebraska.gov/).

## Why it matters

Saturday and Sunday are the two heaviest return-travel days of the Thanksgiving week, when the year's biggest road-travel surge — AAA projected nearly 73 million Americans driving for the holiday, a figure the [Nebraska State Patrol cited in its Thanksgiving enforcement announcement](https://statepatrol.nebraska.gov/troopers-ready-patrol-busy-thanksgiving-travel) — funnels back onto I-80. A half inch of rain is routine weather, but on an interstate carrying holiday loads plus freight through narrowed work zones, it means spray, longer stopping distances and pooling in rutted lanes. The sharper risk is Sunday at dawn: sunshine forecast, pavement that looks dry, and a bridge deck that froze overnight.

Troopers are out in force through Sunday under the *Click It or Ticket* campaign that runs Nov. 23-29 — see our [Thanksgiving patrol story](/news/nsp-runs-click-it-or-ticket-patrols-on-i-80-for-thanksgiving) — and the RIDER impaired-driving task force is working county corridors alongside it. Wet Saturday pavement plus Sunday-morning ice is exactly the setup where seat belts, speed discipline and sober driving matter most.

## How to check before each leg

Recheck the NWS office for your segment — [Omaha](https://www.weather.gov/oax/), [Hastings](https://www.weather.gov/gid/), [North Platte](https://www.weather.gov/lbf/), [Cheyenne](https://www.weather.gov/cys/) — and the live [Nebraska 511 map](https://511.nebraska.gov/) before each leg, not once for the weekend. If you are new to the 511 cameras and event icons, our [guide to reading Nebraska 511](/news/how-to-read-nebraska-511) walks through it, and our [winter-driving guide](/news/winter-driving-i-80-nebraska) covers closures, plows and when to stop. Sunday's outlook — our [Thanksgiving-week snapshot](/news/thanksgiving-week-on-i-80-dry-stretch-cold-nights-four-work-zones-to-watch) has the work-zone details — stays the bigger picture: dry roads return Monday through Wednesday with highs rebounding into the 50s. If a warning goes up or a closure hits, we will update this story; the current snapshot is date-stamped Friday, Nov. 27.
