Does the shape read now?

Panhandle 23% of the width and 67% of the height, Missouri on the east, straight south border. At 48 × 22 the aspect is 2.18:1, which matches the real state.

Grade A

Ridged

Five curved ridges instead of a kernel grid. Reads as corn at 260, as a ribbed gold shape at 64, and as a sliver at 32. The middle option, and the one I would use.

Grade B

Dotted

Eight round kernels, two columns. Less like a photograph, more like a stamp. At 64 the dots merge into a pattern; at 32 they vanish.

Grade C

Silhouette

One colour, no interior at all. The simplest and the most likely to survive a favicon — if it reads as corn, it is only because of the husks and the shape.
