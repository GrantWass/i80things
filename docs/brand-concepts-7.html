One cob, two ways

Same Nebraska path, same badge, same 80. Only the corn changes: bare, or wrapped in husks modelled on how the better corn icons actually draw them — two broad crescents that sweep up and overlap the cob, not blades stuck on the bottom.

Option 1

No husk

Just the cob: tapered body, kernel texture, nothing else. The cleanest shape in the badge and the one that will not fight the state. At 64 px it becomes a gold capsule, which is the trade.

Option 2

With husk

Two broad crescents sweep up the sides and overlap the cob, the way the better corn icons draw them. The leaves are the widest point of the mark, which is what makes it read as corn at 260 — and what crowds the state at 64.
