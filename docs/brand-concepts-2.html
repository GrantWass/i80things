The top band, five ways

Same badge, same palette, same type — everything happens in the empty strip above INTERSTATE (dashed red below, for reference). That band is 24 × 10 units at 64 px: enough for a horizon, a river, three kernels or a state outline, not enough for a scene.

Reference

The empty band

The space you noticed. It is the only part of the badge with nothing to do, which is why every idea below lives there.

Landscape

Big Sky

A low amber sun on a hairline horizon. Nebraska is a big-sky state before it is a corn state, and a dot over a line survives every size down to the favicon.

Reads asA dot on a horizon. Nobody has to know it is intentional.
RiskLow. It could also read as a generic sunrise logo, which is the price of calm.
Pairs withFurrow (set one) — same horizon logic, one for the badge, one for the page.

Geography

The Platte

I-80 follows the Platte for most of the corridor. A thin river line with a small fork above the label says “this place” without naming a crop or a town.

Reads asA river, or just a decorative line. Locals who know the Platte get it instantly.
RiskMedium: squiggles are the universal sign of “wellness brand.” It needs to stay thin and geometric, not hand-drawn.
Pairs withNothing in set one. It is its own move.

Warm

Three Kernels

Three amber kernels in a row above the label — a stylised ear, or an ellipsis for Things. The most obviously Nebraskan of the five, and still three dots at favicon size.

Reads asA warm detail that locals will name. “The corn one.”
RiskMedium: three amber dots can read as a menu or an ellipsis before they read as kernels.
Pairs withKernel Dot (set one) — but do not use both. One corn reference per page.

Place

Nebraska

A 15 × 9 unit silhouette of the state, panhandle notch and all. No crop, no sky, just the shape — the most literal and the most local of the five.

Reads as“That is Nebraska.” Instant for locals, invisible to everyone else.
RiskMedium-high: at 16 px it is an amber blob, and a filled shape this small can look like a stain.
Pairs withNothing. The outline is doing all the work.

Wildcard

Crane

A sandhill crane in the band — the Platte migration is the most Nebraska thing that is not a crop. Elegant, unexpected, and completely gone at small sizes.

Reads asSomething wild and specific. The only concept that implies “this is a place with a season,” not just a crop.
RiskHigh at small sizes. Would need the plain badge under 48 px, and the silhouette has to be redrawn by someone who can draw birds.
Pairs withBig Sky, in an alternate universe where the mark is illustrated rather than geometric.
