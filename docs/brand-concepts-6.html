The traced shape

The cob

The husk cup

Three leaves form a cup around the base of the cob, with the middle leaf in front. The cob sits in the husk instead of balancing on fins, which is what makes it read as corn rather than a rocket.

What changed

The husk now wraps the base instead of radiating from it, and the cob has kernel texture so it reads at 260. At 64 it is still a gold shape with a pale base, which is the honest limit of the size.
